Frequently asked questions

Does JoraSpeaks need an account?
No. Your profile, progress, XP, and streaks are stored locally on your device — there is nothing to sign up for.

Does the app work offline?
Yes. Lessons, word cards, and pronunciation drills are designed to work offline once the app is installed.

Does JoraSpeaks record my voice?
No. The current iOS app does not request microphone or speech-recognition access. Listen-and-repeat exercises use an on-device system voice, and any practice rating is your own self-assessment.

How do I reset my progress?
Since all data is stored on your device, deleting and reinstalling the app resets everything. If your device's cloud backup is enabled, restoring a backup may bring your progress back.

How is my data handled?
Your learning data stays on your device. See the full privacy policy for details.
