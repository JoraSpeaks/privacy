JoraSpeaks. 🐶 Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

JoraSpeaks is a local-first English practice app. This policy describes how the current mobile app build handles your data — the short version is that your learning stays on your device.

✅

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your profile, progress, XP, streaks and preferences are stored locally on your device. Nothing is sold, and there are no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Data we collect

The app stores your learning profile details, progress, XP, streaks, selected level, downloaded lesson state, and app preferences locally on your device.

The app does not sell subscriptions, process payments, or collect payment details.

Speaking practice

The current iOS app does not request microphone or speech-recognition access. It does not record, store, transmit, or analyse your voice. Pronunciation exercises use on-device text-to-speech for listen-and-repeat practice, followed by an optional self-assessment.

Account data

The app uses a local-only profile for onboarding and progress. It does not create a server account.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data only when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to operate services that are clearly described in the app.

Backups

If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your on-device app data may be included in that backup and restored when you set up a new device. This is handled by iOS under Apple's privacy terms; JoraSpeaks does not operate a server that receives this data.

Children

JoraSpeaks is intended for general education use. Children should use the app with parent or guardian supervision where required by local law.

Contact

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected before a new app release is submitted for review.
